Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Data

1. Data Collection

To facilitate life-saving connections, LifeDrop collects personal information such as your Name, Phone Number, Blood Group, and Location data. We collect this data only for the purpose of connecting donors with recipients.

2. How Data is Used

Your details will be displayed to users performing a search in your location for your blood group. By registering, you agree that recipients may contact you using the phone number provided.

3. Data Protection Statement

LifeDrop takes your privacy seriously. We secure your data against unauthorized access and do not sell or share your data with third main-party marketers or advertisers.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions, wish to remove your profile, or need technical support, please contact us at support@lifedrop.example.com.
